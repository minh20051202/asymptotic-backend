## Limitations

- One verifier sub-agent stopped due usage limit, so final synthesis relies on two completed sub-agents plus local source inspection.
- UML is authoritative for diagram semantics, but not for the exact textual specification template. The template recommendation is a practice choice, not a UML requirement.
- Some external methodology sources are paywalled or provided as previews; claims from them are treated as methodology guidance, not binding standards.
